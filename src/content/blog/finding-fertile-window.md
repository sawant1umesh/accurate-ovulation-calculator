Many believe that pregnancy can occur on any day of the menstrual cycle, but biologically, conception is restricted to a narrow timeline known as the **fertile window**. 

## The 6-Day Fertile Window

The fertile window is determined by the biological lifespan of human gametes:
- **Sperm Lifespan:** Up to 5 days. Healthy sperm can survive in the warm, nutrient-rich environment of the cervix and fallopian tubes if fertile cervical mucus is present.
- **Egg Lifespan:** 12 to 24 hours. Once released during ovulation, the egg must be fertilized within this time frame, or it dissolves and is shed during the next period.

Because of this, the fertile window spans a total of **six days**: the five days leading up to ovulation and the day of ovulation itself. Intercourse outside of this window has a statistical conception probability of near zero.

## Probability of Conception by Day

Conception odds vary significantly depending on when intercourse occurs relative to ovulation:
- **5 Days Before Ovulation:** ~10% chance
- **4 Days Before Ovulation:** ~15% chance
- **3 Days Before Ovulation:** ~20% chance
- **2 Days Before Ovulation:** ~26% chance
- **1 Day Before Ovulation:** ~30% chance
- **Ovulation Day:** ~33% chance (Peak)
- **1 Day After Ovulation:** ~5% (rapid decline as the egg loses viability)

Timing intercourse during the two days preceding ovulation offers the highest likelihood of successful fertilization.
