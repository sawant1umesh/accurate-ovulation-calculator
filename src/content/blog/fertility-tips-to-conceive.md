Conception relies on a complex coordination of hormonal levels, timing, and physical health. Implementing evidence-based lifestyle optimizations can improve egg quality, regularize ovulation, and support successful implantation.

## 1. Optimize Intercourse Timing

The most critical factor is timing. Track your cervical mucus or use Ovulation Predictor Kits (OPKs) to identify your fertile window. Aim to have intercourse every other day during your 6-day fertile window, with focus on the 2 days leading up to ovulation. 

## 2. Take a Prenatal Vitamin

Start taking a high-quality prenatal vitamin containing at least **400 mcg of Folic Acid (or Folate)** at least one month before trying to conceive. Folate is crucial for preventing neural tube defects during the first weeks of embryonic development.

## 3. Focus on Fertility Nutrition

Your diet directly impacts egg health and hormone regulation:
- **Choose Complex Carbohydrates:** Opt for whole grains (oats, quinoa) instead of refined carbs, which can cause insulin spikes that disrupt ovulation.
- **Incorporate Healthy Fats:** Monounsaturated fats from avocados, olive oil, and nuts support ovulation.
- **Emphasize Plant Proteins:** Research indicates replacing a portion of animal protein with plant protein (lentils, beans) lowers the risk of ovulatory infertility.

## 4. Limit Caffeine & Avoid Alcohol

High caffeine intake (above 200 mg daily) is associated with delayed conception. Limit coffee to 1-2 cups per day. Avoid alcohol and tobacco entirely, as they directly reduce fertility in both partners and increase risks of early pregnancy loss.
